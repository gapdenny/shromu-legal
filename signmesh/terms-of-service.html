The product

SignMesh seals proof on this iPhone or iPad. A sealed PDF includes a SHA-256 evidence hash and, when the Secure Enclave is available, a device-bound signature. That is a digital record. It is not a court-certified expert report unless an authorized forensic examiner independently verifies it.

No account

SignMesh does not operate user accounts or a cloud of your proofs.

SignMesh Pro (monthly), SignMesh Business (monthly), and SignMesh Lifetime are sold as in-app purchases. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
