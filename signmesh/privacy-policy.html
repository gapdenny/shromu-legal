Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

SignMesh (iOS app, bundle ID app.pocketvault.ios) is an on-device proof vault. There is no SignMesh account and no SignMesh server.

What stays on your device

Photos, signatures, form answers, GPS attached to a proof you capture, SHA-256 hashes, sealed PDFs, and optional Face ID / passcode locks are stored in the app sandbox. SignMesh does not upload this content.

Permissions

  • Camera — capture proof photos and scan template QR codes.
  • Photo Library — attach EXIF from photos you choose.
  • Location when in use — write coordinates into the proof you capture.
  • Face ID / device passcode — unlock Archive and confirm a seal. Biometric templates never leave the device.

Purchases

SignMesh Pro, Business, and Lifetime are billed by Apple through StoreKit. SignMesh does not collect payment card details.

Backups

An encrypted .pvbackup leaves the device only if you export it (AirDrop, Files, or similar).
