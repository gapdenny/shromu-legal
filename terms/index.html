1. What Shromu is

Shromu is a decision-aid that estimates environmental conditions (habitat / weather) that may favour fruiting of selected species. It is a model index, not a map of real mushrooms on the ground, not a species key, and not eat/don’t-eat advice.

2. Mushroom safety & your responsibility (critical)

  • Poisonous mushrooms are everywhere. Edible and toxic species can share the same patch. Shromu’s map does not filter locations to “safe / edible only”.
  • We do not claim that highlighted areas grow only edible mushrooms (or any mushrooms at all). The index scores conditions — it does not guarantee a find or a species.
  • Identification is yours alone. Before eating anything, verify species with trusted sources / experienced foragers. When in doubt — leave it.
  • Not medical or food advice. The app does not replace a doctor, toxicologist, or public-health guidance. If you suspect poisoning, seek emergency care immediately.
  • Laws and land access. Follow foraging rules, reserves, private property, and local bans. Violations are your responsibility.
  • You assume the risk. By using Shromu to plan walks, you accept all risks of foraging, identification, travel, and being outdoors (injury, getting lost, weather, etc.).

3. Model and data limits

  • Forecasts may be wrong, stale, or unavailable (network, cache, habitat pack).
  • Weather providers (e.g. Open-Meteo / WeatherKit) and third-party maps can err.
  • Community pins (if any) are anonymous and unverified — not proof of edibility.
  • In-app species notes are educational, not an identification key.
